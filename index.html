Problem

Learner's Commitment Struggles

Learners often forget about their online courses because they are busy, resulting in incomplete courses and missed learning opportunities. This lack of commitment hinders their progress and desired upskilling outcomes.

Solution

Paper Research

Starting with paper research, I began to draw from research articles on goals, motivation, and achievement. During my exploration, I stumbled upon an eye-opening published research, and in that research, Professor Benjamin discovered that:

"If you're trying to achieve your goal, the more often you monitor your progress and physically record it, the greater the likelihood that you succeed"

The Main Insight

None of the existing features in other apps worked for my participants due to....

Learner's Persona

Challenges and a Fresh Approach

For the past 1 to 2 weeks, I have been trying to find additional solutions for this problem, including scheduling, deadlines, and notifications. However, upon careful consideration and gathering feedback, I have concluded that adding more features may bring more hassle than benefit. Despite having these existing features, they did not effectively help users discipline themselves to learn more. Therefore, I have decided to proceed with tracking learners' progress as my final solution.

What is Potential?

The Potential screen in Driz is a homepage section designed to communicate the brand’s value at a glance. It showcases the key benefits of Driz detergent, giving users a clear understanding of what the product offers and why it stands out before they explore further or make a purchase.

Research Objective

Goal

Improve the feature so it helps users easily explore products, understand their value, and feel encouraged to try or add more items (like pairing detergent with fabric conditioner).

What we wanted to understand
  • User behavior & problems — Where users get confused when choosing products; Why they hesitate to add more or try other variants
  • Clarity of the feature — Which parts are hard to understand; If users clearly see how the feature helps them (e.g., saves money, better cleaning)
  • Product discovery — If users notice other products in the lineup; If the feature helps them consider alternatives or combinations
  • Motivation to buy more — What makes users want to upgrade or add items; What stops them from doing it

Approach: Survey

Why this method?
  • Identify the most important problems — The survey helps us understand what customers struggle with when choosing and using Driz products (like liquid detergent, powder, pods, and fabric conditioner). It shows which problems matter the most so we know what to fix first.
  • Check if the feature is easy to understand and useful — We can measure if people clearly understand the feature, find it helpful, and feel satisfied when using it. This helps us see if the design is working or needs improvement.
  • See if it encourages customers to get more products — The survey helps us find out if the feature makes people want to try other Driz products or add more items (like pairing detergent with fabric conditioner).
  • Get results quickly for decision-making — This method allows us to collect feedback fast, so the team can improve the feature and plan the next steps without delay.

Survey Logistics

Timeline: ~4 weeks

  • 5 days for scoping and approval
  • 4 days for fielding
  • 7 days for analysis & building the report
  • 1 day to present

Tool: Conducted through Qualtrics.

Analyzing Feedback

I exported the open-ended comments into Google Sheets, manually coded them, removed any sensitive identifying information, and then used AI to review the open-ends to ensure no key themes were overlooked (AI peer debriefing).
